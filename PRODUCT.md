## Anti-references

The previous design is rejected. Avoid generic analytics dashboards, repeated identical cards, purple-gradient decoration, glowing orbs, fake terminal decoration, stock developer photography, excessive 3D, inaccessible motion and unsupported performance/customer/revenue claims. Conceptual illustrations must never masquerade as genuine application screenshots.

## Design principles

1. Lead with engineering work and its evidence.
2. Let projects retain their own identity inside a coherent portfolio.
3. Give recruiters a concise overview and engineers an optional deeper reading path.
4. Preserve factual chronology, publication permissions and explicit release status.
5. Prefer static, readable, accessible content over unnecessary runtime infrastructure.
